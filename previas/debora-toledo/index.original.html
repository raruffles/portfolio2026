Our Studio

BCG

Every considered home begins with a considered approach to design.

The difference between an exceptional design and an exceptional home is everything that happens between concept and completion. BCG exists to close that gap, guiding every stage personally so the home that is envisioned is the home that gets built.

BCG is a boutique building design studio based on the Gold Coast, specialising in beautifully designed custom homes and major renovations across South East Queensland and Northern NSW.

Led personally by Bianca Gemmill, every design starts with how your family actually lives. From the studio to the site, that thinking shapes homes that make daily routines effortless, adapt as your family evolves and hold lasting value.
